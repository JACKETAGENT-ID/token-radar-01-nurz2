Methodology — the whole model, nothing hidden

Implied value follows only from the snapshot's own figures. No protocol name is special-cased; the same formula scores every row.

implied = TVL × categoryMultiple × chainMult × growthMult × maturityMult

chainMult    = 1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 10)            → 1.00 – 1.20
growthMult   = 1 + clamp(change_7d, −50, +80) / 100 × 0.7
               (change_7d missing counts as 0 — no growth claimed)
maturityMult = age unknown → 0.85
               else 0.70 + 0.30 × min(age_days, 1095) / 1095 → 0.70 – 1.00

How to read the board

  • Watchlist (40) — the snapshot's own floor: TVL ≥ $50M, records no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of one that trades. The board ranks all 40, plus 138 early protocols below the floor.
  • “Records no token” — the snapshot is DefiLlama's record, not proof. A protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry.
  • Confidence — high: tracked ≥ 1 year, 7-day change reported, on ≥ 3 chains. Medium: tracked ≥ 120 days. Early: anything younger or thinner.
  • Farm plan — 7 steps per protocol, each with a cost and a time. Capital follows the TVL tier ($50–$200); hold length follows age (30–75 days). Never pay a fee to claim.
